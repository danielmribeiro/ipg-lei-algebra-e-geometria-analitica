# Álgebra e Geometria Analítica (Algebra and Analytic Geometry)
Repository for the "Algebra e Geometria Analítica" course at Instituto Politécnico da Guarda (IPG), part of the "Licenciatura em Engenharia Informática" degree program. This repository contains exercises, resources, and materials related to the study of Algebra and Analytical Geometry.


## Course Objectives
Upon completion of this course, students should be able to:
1. Operate with complex numbers.
2. Work with matrices and solve systems of linear equations using matrix methods.
3. Understand and apply concepts related to vector spaces.
4. Master the concept of linear transformations.
5. Solve problems involving vector products and analytic geometry.

## Program Contents
### Complex Numbers
- Algebraic and trigonometric forms
- Operations and properties
- Geometric transformations

### Vector Spaces
- Definition and properties
- Subvector spaces, intersection, union, and sum of subspaces
- Span of a set of vectors
- Linear independence, bases, and dimension

### Matrices
- Matrix algebra and properties
- Matrix condensation and inversion
- Solving systems of linear equations
- Row and column spaces and their bases
- Null space and nullity

### Determinants
- Definition and calculation
- Properties
- Laplace’s theorem
- Inverse matrix
- Rouché’s theorem
- Eigenvalues and eigenvectors

### Linear Transformations
- Definition
- Matrix representation of a linear transformation
- Change of basis matrix
- Algebraic operations
- Null space and codomain
- Characteristic and nullity
- Inverse of a linear transformation

### Vector Products and Analytic Geometry
- Inner, outer, mixed products, and norms
- Area and volume calculations
- Equations of lines and planes
- Metric and non-metric problems

## Learning Methodologies
- The course includes both expository and interactive sessions.
- Students are encouraged to solve exercises under the guidance of the instructor.
- Practical application of theoretical concepts will be emphasized to reinforce learning.

## Assessment Criteria
- **Continuous Assessment**: Three written tests with a minimum score of 4 in each and a final average score of 10 or higher for approval.
- **Final Exam**: Available in normal, recovery, and special periods, requiring a minimum score of 10 for approval.
- **Additional Notes**: 
  - A mandatory oral exam for scores above 16.
  - Tests will be closed-book with restrictions on calculators and mobile phones.
